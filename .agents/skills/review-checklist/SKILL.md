# Review checklist

Read the full brief the user points to, including any scope or rollout sections. Check each item against what the brief itself says.

1. **Owner.** Look for a named person, team, or clearly designated role accountable for the work. A stakeholder mention or a plan to find an owner later leaves ownership open.
2. **Success measure.** Look for an observable outcome and a concrete way to judge whether the work achieved it. A list of features or deliverables leaves success undefined.
3. **Consistent scope.** Compare the opening commitment with the later requirements, deliverables, and rollout. Flag additions or contradictions that change what the brief commits to. Clearly labeled future work can sit outside the current scope.
4. **Problem before fix.** Check that the brief explains who is affected and what is going wrong before it pitches or describes a solution. A title naming the proposal is fine if the body establishes the problem first.
